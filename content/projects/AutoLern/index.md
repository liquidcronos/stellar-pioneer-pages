I joined AutoLern toward the later phase of the project to support the application of machine learning in production environments. My contribution focused on helping translate research results into deployable solutions that could be integrated into industrial workflows rather than remaining isolated prototype models.

In particular, I worked on questions around deploying machine learning models on real systems, where robustness, maintainability, and interaction with existing automation infrastructure matter just as much as model quality. This work complemented the broader project goal of enabling self-learning machine tools that can assess model validity during operation and adapt to changing boundary conditions.
